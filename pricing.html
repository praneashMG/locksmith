Frequently Asked

Is there a call‑out fee?

No. We charge a flat service fee that covers travel within 10 miles. Beyond that, a small mileage fee applies.

2

Yes, emergency service between 10 PM and 6 AM has a modest surcharge. We always tell you first.

3

Yes — show us a written quote from a licensed competitor and we'll match it if possible.
